COVID-19 Global Impact Dashboard — Power BI DAX Measures

Assume the main table is named Covid.

1. Total Cases = SUM(Covid[total_cases])
2. Total Deaths = SUM(Covid[total_deaths])
3. New Cases = SUM(Covid[new_cases])
4. New Deaths = SUM(Covid[new_deaths])
5. Cases per Million = DIVIDE([Total Cases], MAX(Covid[population])) * 1000000
6. Deaths per Million = DIVIDE([Total Deaths], MAX(Covid[population])) * 1000000
7. Vaccinated % = DIVIDE(MAX(Covid[people_vaccinated]), MAX(Covid[population]))
8. Case Fatality Rate = DIVIDE([Total Deaths], [Total Cases])
9. Avg Daily Cases / M = AVERAGE(Covid[new_cases_per_million])
10. Avg Daily Deaths / M = AVERAGE(Covid[new_deaths_per_million])

Suggested visuals:
- KPI cards: Total Cases, Total Deaths, Vaccinated %, Case Fatality Rate
- Line chart: Date on X-axis; cases_7d_avg_per_million and deaths_7d_avg_per_million as values
- Filled map: Country = location; Cases per Million or Deaths per Million as color saturation
- Bar chart: Top 10 countries by total_cases_per_million
- Bar chart: Top 10 countries by total_deaths_per_million
- Scatter: Vaccinated % vs Deaths per Million, Location as legend/details
- Slicers: Country, Date, Continent, Year

Power BI modeling tip:
Create a Date table:
DateTable = CALENDAR(MIN(Covid[date]), MAX(Covid[date]))
Then add Year = YEAR(DateTable[Date]), Month = FORMAT(DateTable[Date], "YYYY-MM"), and relate DateTable[Date] to Covid[date].
